## Remaining acceptance limits

- Native mountpoint retirement and same-device bind-mount behavior require the unavailable mount fixture. The configured nested-mount copy test covers refusal and explicit traversal when enabled.
- ENOSPC and quota errors are injected into the actual buffered copy loop. A real full or quota-limited destination filesystem remains unavailable.
- The full-engine method-parity test verifies native and buffered outputs. Successful Linux/macOS clone and native macOS preservation still require capable hosts.
- Native allocation behavior on a filesystem without sparse support remains unverified. The diagnostic test forces dense storage on a capable filesystem and checks reported block accounting.
- P3 acceptance remains incomplete. Local exploratory measurements and their resource limits are recorded in `benches/fs/results-linux.md`; unavailable native comparisons are not passing evidence.
